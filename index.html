01 THE METHOD

Three decisions.
One compact form.

SAWT does not try to redraw English letter-for-letter. It asks what has to survive for the word to remain recognisable at speed.

02 SOUND MAP

The original marks,
with room to evolve.

The photographed marks are preserved as Observed. Vowel tones are shown attached above a sample consonant, matching the placement rule in your word examples. Missing English sounds remain Proposed.

No marks match that search.

Extension rule

New sounds should cost one tiny gesture.

Each proposal is designed to remain visually distinct at writing speed. Accent-sensitive sounds can be merged later if you want SAWT to optimize for Indian English rather than a broad English phoneme inventory.

04

TR[AI]ING→TR[AI]+→

TRYING uses the AI tone, not AY. AI is attached to R, so the AI tone is written above R; the existing + symbol then replaces the -ING ending.
